Enhance the existing Wine Pairing app so users can click/tap on ANY dish card or wine card to view full details.

DO NOT redesign the entire UI.
DO NOT change theme colors or typography.
Add interactive detail views cleanly and consistently.

----------------------------------
GOAL
----------------------------------
1) Clicking a Dish card opens a detailed view.
2) Clicking a Wine card opens a detailed view.
3) Must work on mobile and desktop.
4) Smooth animation.
5) No layout breaking or text overflow.

----------------------------------
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------

DISH CARD CLICK BEHAVIOR:
- Make the entire card clickable (cursor pointer on hover for web).
- On click:
   - Open a modal (desktop) OR slide-up drawer (mobile).
- Show:
   - Dish name
   - Category
   - Full description
   - Extracted attributes (protein, sauce, spice level, richness)
   - Top 3 wine pairings
   - Pairing explanation for each wine
   - “Close” button (X top right)

WINE CARD CLICK BEHAVIOR:
- Entire wine card clickable.
- On click:
   - Open modal/drawer.
- Show:
   - Wine name + vintage
   - Region + grape(s)
   - Price tier badge ($-$$$$)
   - Style breakdown (body, acidity, tannin, sweetness, oak)
   - Full tasting notes (aroma bullets + palate bullets)
   - Best food matches
   - Serving temperature suggestion
   - “Close” button
